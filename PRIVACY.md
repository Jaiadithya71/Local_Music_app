# Privacy Policy

VibeStream is a personal, offline-first music app. It has no servers of its own and collects nothing.

- Your music library, downloads, likes, playlists and listening history are stored only on your device.
- VibeStream shows no ads, uses no analytics, and shares no data with third parties.
- Search and playback talk to YouTube / YouTube Music directly from your device; those requests are governed by Google's own privacy policy.
- Optional YouTube Music sign-in stores only Google's sign-in token, encrypted on your device. It is sent only to Google, and signing out deletes it locally and revokes it with Google.
- Library backups are files you export yourself; they go only where you choose to save them.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
