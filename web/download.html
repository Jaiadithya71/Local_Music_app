Phone-First Architecture
⚡
Smart Playback Routing: When you pick a song, it instantly checks if it is saved to local storage (`file://`). If yes, it plays from local storage without streaming data.
🔍
Live Autocomplete Engine: Real-time search recommendations query as you type on your keyboard with automated 220ms debounce.
📱
System Lock Screen & Media Controls: Uses the Media3 playback service; test this on your device.
🛡️
Privacy & Scoped Storage: No sensitive permissions required (no camera, contacts, or location). Offline songs are safely compartmentalized in app-specific storage.
